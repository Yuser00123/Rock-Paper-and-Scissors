Information and rules

There are some rules for this game:

  • The player who wins the most rounds wins the game.
  • Rock beats scissors, paper beats rock and scissors beats paper.
  • For every round you win, you will get a point.
  • For every round you lose, AI will get a point.
  • No points for tie rounds.
  • To quit the game, enter "quit" in the input box.

Some info you should know:

  • You will play against an real AI opponent.
  • The AI predicts your next move based on previous moves.
  • The AI will start predicting moves after 7 rounds.
  • There is also a surprise for you when you win the match.
